Compress a PDF

Shrink scanned or image-heavy PDFs by lowering image quality.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose or drop a PDF file.
  2. Pick a method and level. Reduce image quality gives the biggest savings.
  3. Click Compress PDF and compare the sizes before you download.

Good to know

Will compressing change how my PDF looks?

With Reduce image quality, pages become lower-resolution images, so fine detail may soften and text can no longer be selected. Optimize only changes nothing you can see.
