Rotate PDF pages

Rotate single pages or the whole document.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose or drop a PDF file.
  2. Use the arrows under a page to rotate it, or rotate every page at once.
  3. Click Save rotated PDF and download the result.

Good to know

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes. Each page has its own rotate buttons, so you can turn just the pages that need it.
