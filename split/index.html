Split a PDF

Split into single pages, custom ranges or equal parts. Multiple files download as a ZIP.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose or drop a PDF file.
  2. Pick how to split: every page, custom ranges, or every N pages.
  3. Click Split PDF. Several files download together as a ZIP.

Good to know

Can I split a PDF by page ranges?

Yes. Choose Page ranges and type something like 1-3, 4-6, 9. Each range becomes its own PDF.
